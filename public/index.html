The circuit

Race three laps to the finish. The top six score 10 / 8 / 6 / 4 / 2 / 1 points. A Grand Prix continues across three tracks chosen from eight hand-built circuits; the highest total takes the cup. All six vehicles have identical speed and handling. Your style is the only difference.

Find your controls

Six friends can share one keyboard — each slot lives in its own corner of the board — plus one gamepad. Tap a slot button in the garage to claim it.
